Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

The DynamicNotch apps have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Almost everything happens on your Mac and your iPhone or iPad. What does leave them is listed below: your license check, song lookups for lyrics, and, if you use the iPhone or iPad app, messages between your Mac and your phone, which are encrypted so that nobody else, including us, can read them. Our website counts visits and downloads with Google Analytics, and only sets its cookies if you agree.

This policy covers DynamicNotch for Mac and DynamicNotch Companion for iPhone and iPad (together, “DynamicNotch”). “We” means the developer of DynamicNotch.

What stays on your devices

  • The island. What's playing, your volume and brightness, battery, Bluetooth devices and their battery levels, Focus, downloads, timers, temperatures and fan speeds are read on your Mac and shown on your Mac.
  • Camera and microphone indicators. DynamicNotch notices which app is using your camera or microphone. It never sees or hears what they capture.
  • Live waveform. If you turn it on, DynamicNotch measures how loud the music playing on your Mac is, to move the bars. Nothing is recorded or stored.
  • Clipboard and file conversion. Files you drop on the island, and files you convert, stay on your Mac. Conversions happen on your Mac; nothing is uploaded.
  • Claude and ChatGPT. If you connect them, they tell DynamicNotch on your Mac when they're working, need a permission, ask a question or finish. DynamicNotch shows only what that needs: the project's folder name, the command or question, and the last line of a finished task, which it keeps in a short Recent list on your Mac (and your paired iPhone or iPad). It stays on your devices, except as described below for the iPhone and iPad app.
  • Pairing. Pairing your iPhone or iPad with your Mac creates a secret key that's kept on both (in the Keychain on iPhone and iPad), and never sent to us.
  • The camera on iPhone and iPad is used only to scan your Mac's pairing code. No photos are taken or kept.

What leaves your devices

Your license (Mac)

When you buy DynamicNotch, payment is handled by Stripe; we never see your card. Our license server keeps your license key, plan, the email address you gave at checkout, your Stripe customer and subscription references, and an identifier for each Mac you activate. That identifier is a one-way hash, not your Mac's serial number or name. Your Mac sends its license key and this identifier to the license server to activate, and checks in about twice a day to keep your license current. We use this only to provide and verify your license.

Lyrics (Mac)

If lyrics are on, the title, artist, album and length of the song you're playing are sent to LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a free lyrics library, to find its words.

Updates (Mac)

Your Mac checks our website for new versions of DynamicNotch. Like any web page, that request includes your IP address and basic information about your Mac's system, which the website's host (GitHub) receives.

Your iPhone or iPad and your Mac

  • On the same network, your iPhone or iPad talks to your Mac directly. Everything is encrypted with your pairing key.
  • Away from home (DynamicNotch Pro), messages go through our server, which passes them between your Mac and your phone. They're encrypted with your pairing key before they leave either device, so the server can't read them. It keeps only a one-way hash of a secret derived from that key, to let in only your devices.
  • When the app is closed, your Mac can update your Live Activity or send a notification. To do that, your iPhone or iPad gives your Mac its push tokens (Apple's addresses for your device), and your Mac sends the update through our server to Apple's Push Notification service. Such an update holds what the notification shows, such as “Claude · Permission: Run npm test” and the project's folder name. Our server passes it to Apple at once and doesn't store it.

Service providers

Our license server runs on Cloudflare, which handles its traffic, including your IP address, as any web host does. We don't keep logs of your requests beyond brief error messages.

Our website

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address and browser details when you visit, as any web host does.

To see how many people visit it and download DynamicNotch, the website uses Google Analytics. When you first visit, it asks whether you agree:

  • If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies and tells Google which pages you visit, when you download the app, the site that sent you, your browser and device type, and an approximate location (country or city) derived from your IP address. Google doesn't share your full IP address with us, and we don't use any of this for advertising.
  • If you decline, or haven't chosen yet, no analytics cookies are set. Google may still receive a basic signal that a page was viewed, without cookies or anything that identifies you over time (Google calls this Consent Mode).

You can change your answer at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of each page. Google handles this data under its privacy policy. The website stores your answer in your browser, not on any server.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share your information, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites. The DynamicNotch apps contain no analytics or advertising code; the website uses Google Analytics only as described above.
  • We don't see your files, your screen, your conversations with Claude or ChatGPT, or anything you type. The only exception is the short text of a notification passing through our server on its way to Apple, as above, which isn't stored.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your license is kept for as long as it's active, so you can keep using it and move it between Macs. To free a Mac, use Settings → License → Deactivate this Mac. To have your license information deleted, contact us at the address below. Removing the apps removes everything they keep on your devices. On your Mac, that's ~/Library/Application Support/DynamicNotch; on iPhone and iPad, deleting the app does it.

Children

DynamicNotch isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.
