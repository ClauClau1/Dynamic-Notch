  1. Download DynamicNotch

    It needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

    Open the download and drag DynamicNotch into your Applications folder.

  3. Open it

    The island appears in your notch, or in a notch DynamicNotch draws for you. Click it to open, and find Settings in the menu bar.
