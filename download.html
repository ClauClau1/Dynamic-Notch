Install it

  1. Open DynamicNotch.dmg from your Downloads.
  2. Drag DynamicNotch into the Applications folder.
  3. Open it. The island appears in your notch, or in a notch DynamicNotch draws on Macs without one. Settings live in the menu bar.

Your free day

Everything works for 24 hours from the first time you open it. After that, pick a plan to keep the island: monthly, yearly with a week free, or lifetime.

Already have a key? Open Settings → License from the menu bar and click Activate.

What you need

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
  • Any Mac: on a MacBook with a notch, the island grows out of it; on other Macs it draws its own.

Permissions it may ask for

  • Bluetooth, to show AirPods and their battery when they connect.
  • Focus, to show when a Focus turns on or off.
  • Music and Spotify, to show and control what's playing.
  • Your password, only if you turn on fan control, to install its small helper.

Removing it

If you turned on fan control, first click Uninstall… in Settings → Fans so the fans go back to macOS. Then quit DynamicNotch from the menu bar and drag it from Applications to the Trash. To use your license on another Mac, click Deactivate this Mac in Settings → License before you remove it.
